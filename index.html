I'm a PhD student in Operations Research at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.

I study the mathematical foundations of learning and decision-making, especially in partially observable settings. I enjoy questions that connect theory with the realities of sequential data: what a model can identify, what an agent can know, and how to act when uncertainty cannot be eliminated.
